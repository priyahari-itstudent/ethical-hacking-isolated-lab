# Project 03 – Lab Log

## Objective
To perform basic reconnaissance of an authorised local
OWASP Juice Shop lab and document its attack surface.

## Lab Details
- Target: http://127.0.0.1:3000
- Application: OWASP Juice Shop
- Version: 20.3.0
- Node.js: 26.13.0
- Port: 3000

## Activities Completed
1. Started the application on localhost.
2. Opened the application in the browser.
3. Observed the homepage and product listing.
4. Checked product details and reviews.
5. Observed search, login, basket and language features.
6. Inspected the main HTTP request.
7. Recorded the GET method and 304 status.
8. Observed the X-Content-Type-Options header.

## Result
Basic local reconnaissance was completed.
Observations and potential risk hypotheses were
documented in inventory.md.

## Scope
All activities were limited to the authorised
localhost lab environment.
